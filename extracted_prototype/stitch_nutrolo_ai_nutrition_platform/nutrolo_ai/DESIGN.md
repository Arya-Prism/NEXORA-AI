## Brand & Style
The design system positions the brand as an intelligent, clinical-grade, yet deeply approachable personal health companion. It avoids clinical coldness through botanical warmth while rejecting playful wellness tropes in favor of executive-tier analytical rigor. The target audience comprises health-conscious professionals, biohackers, and everyday individuals seeking actionable nutrition intelligence without cognitive overload.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Organic SaaS** with disciplined **Minimalism and Soft Glassmorphism**:
- **Purity & Breathability:** Generous atmospheric negative space anchors dense nutritional analytics, creating a serene, cognitive-restorative canvas.
- **Organic Precision:** Deep forest undertones paired with crisp emerald and mint accents simulate biological vitality, precision agriculture, and scientific clarity.
- **Physical Luminance:** Subtle translucent backdrops, whisper-thin structural borders, and delicate ambient glows mimic clean laboratory glassware under natural daylight.

## Colors
The palette balances authoritative deep botanicals with high-energy biometrics and crisp, restorative base neutrals.

### Key Palettes & Roles
- **Primary (`#144331` - Deep Forest):** Represents clinical authority, grounding surfaces, primary actions, and primary text headlines. Supported by `#0F3828` for pressed/focused interactive states and `#1C5941` for elevated hover tiers.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Vital Emerald):** Represents metabolic balance, goal achievements, vital status metrics, and high-priority interactive affordances. Supported by `#059669` for deep active indicators and `#34D399` for progress tracks and positive delta trends.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Solar Amber):** Allocated strictly for energy, carbohydrate/calorie metrics, and cautionary thresholds. Paired with auxiliary functional accents: `#EF4444` (Lipids/Critical Alerts) and `#6366F1` (Hydration/Micronutrient synthesis).
- **Neutral Canvas & Tints:**
  - Base canvas: `#FFFFFF` and off-white layered wash `#F8FAF9`.
  - Surface Containers: `#F0F4F2` for muted structural modules.
  - Botanical Washes: `#E6F4EA` and `#D1E7DD` for soft chip foundations, pill badges, and active contextual highlights.
  - Slate Stroke: `rgba(20, 67, 49, 0.08)` and `#E2E8F0` for hairline dividers.

## Typography
The system employs an authoritative structural pairing: **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides humanistic geometric geometry for titles and metrics, while **Inter** delivers maximum legibility across dense data tables, nutrition labels, and conversational AI transcripts.

### Hierarchy & Rules
- **Numerical Tabular Alignment:** All nutrient counters, gram distributions, and macro percentages enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` to prevent jitter during real-time caloric recalibration.
- **Metric Numbers:** Defined via `metric-num` for high-impact dashboard KPIs, retaining optical tracking parity with display headlines.
- **Micro Labels:** Badges, unit annotations, and nutrient targets use `label-sm` set to uppercase with wide tracking (`0.04em`) to maintain sharp contrast against translucent substrates.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a fluid 12-column grid system bound by maximum dashboard container constraints, adapting seamlessly from high-density desktop monitoring to modular mobile cards.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, `margin-lg` (3rem / 48px) boundaries, `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px). Maximum container width is constrained to `1440px` to maintain optimal data scanning sightlines.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, `margin` (1.5rem / 24px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Sidebar collapses into an anchored persistent dock or slide drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-sm` (1rem / 16px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px). Analytics charts stack vertically, and metric rings convert into linear proportional split bars.
- **Modular Cadence:** Padding follows an absolute 8pt scale (`space-xs` through `space-xl`). Card interior padding uses `space-lg` (24px) for desktop analytics and scales down to `space-md` (16px) on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth relies on crystalline atmospheric stacking rather than heavy visual drops. The system simulates optical refraction across light biological surfaces using diffuse green-tinted shadows and fine borders.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Pure `#F8FAF9` with ambient radial color gradients (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.04)`) localized beneath primary analytical cards.
- **Level 1 (Glassmorphic Cards):** `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, bordered by a 1px continuous stroke of `rgba(20, 67, 49, 0.08)`. Shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(20, 67, 49, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Interactive Cards & Popovers):** `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, bordered by `rgba(20, 67, 49, 0.12)`. Shadow: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(20, 67, 49, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(20, 67, 49, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Command Floating Panels):** Pure solid `#FFFFFF`, bordered by `rgba(20, 67, 49, 0.15)`. Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 56, 40, 0.16)`.

## Shapes
The design language adopts a welcoming, approachable, high-curvature aesthetic. Containers utilize modern generous radii that soften quantitative data without compromising modular density.

### Radius Distribution
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Use `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) up to 2rem (32px) on prominent feature hero widgets to establish soft physical paperweight forms.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Selectors, Table rows):** Use standard rounded tokens (0.5rem / 8px to 0.75rem / 12px) to preserve mechanical precision.
- **Badges, Filter Pills, & Metric Status Dots:** Use full circular bounds (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to establish visual contrast against structural content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#144331`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.75rem`. Hover triggers `#1C5941` alongside a subtle box-shadow of `0 4px 12px rgba(20, 67, 49, 0.2)`. Active transitions to `#0F3828`.
- **Secondary / Action:** Solid Emerald `#10B981` with white typography for metabolic achievements and conversion pathways. Hover triggers `#059669`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#144331`, hover background `rgba(230, 244, 234, 0.6)`.

### Glassmorphism Cards & Panels
- Constructed with `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined by a crisp 1px border `rgba(20, 67, 49, 0.08)`. Corner radius fixed at `1.5rem` (`rounded-2xl`).

### Chips & Soft Pill Badges
- Pill geometry (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Success/Macro:** Background `#E6F4EA`, text `#0F3828`, optional leading 6px emerald indicator pulse.
- **Energy/Warning:** Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#B45309`.
- **Micro-Nutrient/Info:** Background `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.1)`, text `#4338CA`.

### Nutrient Progress Rings & Gauges
- Circular vector displays using non-standard metabolic track styling: Base track `rgba(20, 67, 49, 0.06)`, active dynamic stroke rendered with rounded stroke-caps.
- Micro-gauges use nested concentric rings: Outer Ring (Calories / Emerald), Middle Ring (Proteins / Forest), Inner Ring (Hydration / Indigo).

### Interactive Segmented Tabs
- Pill track container with `#F0F4F2` surface, inner padding `0.25rem`.
- Active segment: `#FFFFFF` pill with elevation micro-shadow `0 2px 4px rgba(20, 67, 49, 0.06)`, text `#144331`, weight `600`. Inactive tab text `#6B7280`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `0.75rem`.
- Focus state: Border color `#10B981`, outline `3px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`. Floating metric units (e.g., `kcal`, `g`, `ml`) set in `Inter` body-sm with `#9CA3AF`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `1.25rem` with `rounded-md` (checkbox) or `rounded-full` (radio). Unchecked: Border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Checked: Solid `#144331` with `#10B981` inner check or dot accent.
